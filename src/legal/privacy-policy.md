# Collab Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Collab collects, uses, and protects your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account data:** name, email, college, course, year, skills
- **Execution Execution Profile data:** optional details you choose to provide
- **Venture data:** venture concepts, descriptions, files, progress updates
- **Usage data:** activity timestamps, feature usage, analytics

## 2. How We Use Information
We use your data to:
- provide and improve the platform
- enable team collaboration
- show venture progress and validations
- maintain security and prevent abuse

## 3. Visibility & Privacy Controls
- Contact details are hidden by default.
- They are visible only to team leads during join requests and to team contributors after joining.
- Venture visibility is controlled by you (college-only or public where available).

## 4. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We only share data:
- with your explicit permission
- with teammates as required for collaboration
- with service providers strictly to run the platform

## 5. Data Retention
We retain data as long as needed to provide services or as required by law. You can request deletion.

## 6. Security
We use access controls, encryption in transit (HTTPS), and role-based permissions to protect data.

## 7. Your Rights
You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing **helpdesk@collab.qzz.io**.

## 8. Changes
We may update this policy. Continued use means acceptance of changes.

## 9. Contact
For privacy questions: **helpdesk@collab.qzz.io**.

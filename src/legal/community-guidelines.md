# Collab Community Guidelines

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026

Collab is a professional space for collaboration. These guidelines protect creators and promote trust.

## 1. Respect & Professionalism
Treat others respectfully. Harassment or abuse is not allowed.

## 2. No Idea Theft
Plagiarism, misrepresentation, or copying without permission will result in suspension or removal.

## 3. Honest Collaboration
Only claim work you contributed to. All contributions should be recorded accurately.

## 4. Safe Communication
Do not share sensitive personal details publicly. Use built‑in collaboration tools.

## 5. Reporting
Report violations to **helpdesk@collab.qzz.io**.

# Collab IP & Ownership Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026

Collab is built to protect creators. This policy explains ownership rights and how we treat intellectual property.

## 1. You Own Your Work
All venture concepts, venture content, files, designs, code, and submissions remain the property of the user or team who created them.

## 2. Collab’s Limited License
To operate the platform, you grant Collab a limited license to host and display your content **only** for collaboration, validation, and platform features.

We do **not** use your venture concepts for any purpose outside the platform.

## 3. Team Collaboration Rights
When you invite teammates, you authorize them to view and contribute to the venture. Ownership remains with the team’s creators and contributors, not Collab.

## 4. No Venture Concept Theft Policy
Collab has a zero-tolerance policy for plagiarism, misrepresentation, or venture concept theft. Violators may be permanently banned.

## 5. Proof of Contribution
Venture timelines and activity logs are recorded to provide proof of contribution if disputes arise.

## 6. Reporting Infringement
If you believe your IP has been infringed, email **helpdesk@joincollab.org**. We will investigate and take appropriate action.

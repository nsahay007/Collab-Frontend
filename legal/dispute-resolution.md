# Collab Dispute Resolution Policy

**Effective Date:** April 1, 2026

This policy outlines how Collab handles disputes related to ownership, contributions, or conduct.

## 1. Reporting a Dispute
Email **helpdesk@collab.qzz.io** with:
- project name
- involved parties
- description of the dispute
- any proof of contribution

## 2. Investigation
Collab will review activity logs, timestamps, and submitted evidence. We may contact all parties for clarification.

## 3. Possible Outcomes
- content removal
- account warnings or suspension
- project access restrictions

## 4. Final Decision
Collab’s decision is based on platform logs and submitted proof. We will communicate outcomes to all parties.
